About Price Elasticity

Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) measures how responsive the quantity demanded is to a change in price.

Formula:

PED = (% Change in Quantity) / (% Change in Price)

Types:

  • Elastic (|PED| > 1): Demand is sensitive to price changes
  • Inelastic (|PED| < 1): Demand is not very sensitive to price changes
  • Unit Elastic (|PED| = 1): Proportional change in demand and price
  • Perfectly Inelastic (PED = 0): Demand doesn't change with price
  • Perfectly Elastic (PED = ∞): Any price change causes infinite demand change
